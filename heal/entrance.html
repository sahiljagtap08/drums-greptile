The product that improves itself.

Drums watches how real users behave, notices when your product fails them — even when nothing throws an error — reproduces it, has a coding agent repair it, and refuses to say fixed until the same user behavior actually succeeds.

View the live incident record
notices

One snippet. Errors, 5xx responses, and silent friction — rage clicks that never touch a log — become incidents with full evidence.

repairs

Every incident is reproduced against HEAD in an isolated worktree, then handed to a coding agent with the evidence and the product's memory.

verifies

The exact user interaction is replayed against the rebooted candidate. Green tests are not enough. The user's failure has to be gone.
